Common questions

Why are no departures showing right now?

Most Tokyo and Kanto rail lines stop running between roughly 00:30 and 05:00 JST. After the last train, departure boards stay empty until the first train of the next service day.

Where does the transit data come from?

Schedules, departures, stations, and bus routes come from the Public Transportation Open Data Center (ODPT). Maps, station search, and walking times come from Apple Maps. Delay alerts come from ODPT and public Kanto rail status feeds.

Do I need to turn on Location Services?

No. Location is optional and only used while the app is open to center the map and sort nearby stations and bus stops. Everything else works without it.

How do favorites and widgets work?

Pin stations or bus stops in the app to check departures faster and show them in NTA's Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Favorites stay on your iPhone.

How do I switch between English and Japanese?

Open the More tab, tap App settings, and pick English or 日本語. You can also change Light or Dark appearance there.
