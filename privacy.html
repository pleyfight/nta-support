NTA Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Support 日本語

The short version

Your favorites and settings stay on your iPhone. NTA has no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Location is optional and only used while the app is open to find nearby stations, bus stops, and walking times. Transit lookups go through our proxy with rounded coordinates and no request logs.

01 Who makes NTA

NTA is an iPhone app made by The Rejecktz (Micheal James Fergerson), an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at codingwithmjf@gmail.com.

02 What NTA saves on your iPhone

NTA saves only what it needs to keep your transit board and widgets working:

  • Favorite rail stations and bus stops
  • Optional Home and Work station picks and preferred commute time
  • Language choice (English or Japanese), appearance mode, haptic setting, and last map center
  • Cached station lists and timetable reference data so the app opens quickly

This data stays on your iPhone in iOS UserDefaults and an on-device App Group (group.com.therejecktz.NTA) so the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets stay in sync. It is never uploaded to a user profile or developer database.

03 Location and network requests

If you allow location access while using the app, NTA reads your coordinates to center the map and sort nearby stations, bus stops, and walking ETAs. Location is never linked to your identity or used for tracking. The app works without location permission.

Timetable, departure, and nearby transit lookups go through the NTA transit proxy on Cloudflare Workers to the Public Transportation Open Data Center (ODPT). Requests use Apple App Attest to verify they come from a genuine copy of NTA without an account or user ID. Coordinates for nearby lookups are rounded before leaving the proxy, and the proxy writes no request logs.

Map tiles, station search, and walking times use Apple Maps (MapKit) under Apple's privacy policy. Delay and disruption banners come from ODPT, Tokyo Metro status JSON, and public Kanto delay feeds.

When you open an official operator map or Support from inside the app, NTA opens the HTTPS page in iOS Safari View (SFSafariViewController).

04 What NTA does not collect

NTA does not ask for or collect accounts, logins, names, email addresses, or phone numbers inside the app.

There are no ad networks, no IDFA tracking (NSPrivacyTracking is off), no third-party analytics SDKs, and no access to your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

05 Sharing and selling

We do not sell your information, share it for advertising, or pass it to data brokers. Network requests go only to the transit, map, and status services needed for the station, bus stop, or route you ask for.

06 Storage, security, and crash reports

On-device data stays in iOS sandboxed storage protected by your device passcode and encryption. Requests to the NTA proxy, ODPT, and Apple Maps use HTTPS.

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may send us anonymized crash reports so we can fix bugs.

07 Your choices

  • Turn off location at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > NTA.
  • Edit or remove saved stations, bus stops, Home, or Work inside the app.
  • Delete all local app and widget data by deleting NTA from your iPhone.

08 Regional privacy rights

If you have a privacy question or request, email codingwithmjf@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

In Japan (APPI / 個人情報保護法), NTA does not acquire personal information such as your name or account profile and does not provide personal data to third parties.

In California (CCPA and CPRA), we do not sell or share personal information and do not collect sensitive personal information beyond optional on-demand location while you use the app.

In the EEA and United Kingdom (GDPR), location and network requests happen only to answer the transit lookups you trigger. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

09 Children

NTA is a general-audience transit app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
